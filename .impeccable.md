# Impeccable Design Context — Eclipse Bar

## Design Context

### Users
High-end cocktail bar patrons in Tel Aviv — curious, design-literate adults booking reservations or discovering the venue for the first time. Viewed on mobile in evening/nighttime contexts (pre-night-out) and desktop at leisure. The experience must feel exclusive and atmospheric, not clinical.

### Brand Personality
**Nocturnal. Theatrical. Precise.**
A celestial shift — light into shadow. Every moment at Eclipse exists once and never again. The brand is deliberate, secretive, and sensory. It rewards attention.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Deep dark (near-black `#08080a`), warm gold accent `#F4C485`, white text
- **Typography**: Mountella display (mapped to Fraunces in Figma) paired with Supreme body (mapped to Inter in Figma)
- **Motion**: Slow, deliberate reveals; parallax; no bounce/elastic
- **Anti-references**: Generic hospitality templates, bright/colorful menus, cluttered layouts
- **Visual language**: Editorial luxury — generous whitespace, dramatic typography scale, subtle grain overlay, celestial imagery

### Design Principles
1. **One night only** — nothing repeats; every interaction feels like a single precious moment
2. **Restraint over decoration** — silence and space are as important as content
3. **Precision in every detail** — spacing, type, motion all feel intentional and considered
4. **Dark and warm** — the palette is deep dark with warm gold, never cold or clinical
5. **Mobile-first nocturnal** — most visitors discover on mobile at night; the experience must feel perfect at 375px

### Quality Bar
Flagship. This is a portfolio/brand centrepiece. Every pixel counts.
